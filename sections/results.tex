\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

We evaluate two outcomes of cross-granularity pretraining: the tissue information retained in each representation and the predictive relationships learned between representations. We first establish anatomical transfer to held-out sections, then examine how cross-granularity learning enriches developmental information while retaining distinct anatomical uses. We finally test transformations at trained and unseen target granularities and use their predicted responses to characterize spatial context dependence.
