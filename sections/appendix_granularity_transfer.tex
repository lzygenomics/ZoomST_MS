\section{Cross-granularity prediction}
\label{app:granularity-transfer}

\paragraph{Representation states.}
The final MOSTA model specified in Section~\ref{sec:experiments} supplies native 256-dimensional molecular states without standardization. Data use follows Table~\ref{tab:evaluation_protocol}.

\paragraph{Same-location state prediction.}
For every position in each section, define $m_i^T=d^{-1}\|T_{a\to b}(e_{i,a})-e_{i,b}\|_2^2$ and $m_i^0=d^{-1}\|e_{i,a}-e_{i,b}\|_2^2$. The unchanged source is the no-transformation reference; the target is directly encoded from the actual broader neighborhood at the same location. MSE averages over all positions in each section. Across all 24 held-out section--direction conditions, transformed states have lower target MSE than unchanged sources.

Figures~\ref{fig:granularity-query-summary}a and~\ref{fig:granularity-transfer}a show the corresponding absolute MSEs. Each paired point is one section's mean over the three trained relations, with each relation first averaged over all positions; the eight section means form each box.

\paragraph{Projected joint association.}
Each section supplies the same fixed 2,048 positions, partitioned into four disjoint batches of 512. For each batch, correct predicted pairings and 20 shuffled predictions are compared with directly encoded fine--coarse pairs. Each shuffle has no fixed points and permutes only predicted destinations, preserving their marginal distribution. The product kernel uses source and target bandwidths from the corresponding observed states, shared across controls, with the settings in Appendix~\ref{app:hyperparameters}.

We evaluate the checkpoint's training projection and eight fixed orthogonal projections. Correct source--prediction pairings yield lower joint discrepancy than shuffled pairings across all evaluated batch--projection--direction conditions. Figure~\ref{fig:granularity-transfer}b compares absolute MMDs, first averaged over batches and the eight fresh projections within each section and direction, then equally over the three trained directions within each section.
