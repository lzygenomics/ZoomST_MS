\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}
\label{sec:backbone}

In this section, we present the core architecture of ZoomST, illustrated in Figure~\ref{fig:method}. ZoomST is pretrained on nested neighborhoods constructed at multiple spatial scales from spatial transcriptomic data. It combines molecular--spatial representation learning at each scale with structured cross-scale transformations.

\subsection{Multi-Granularity Input from Nested Neighborhoods}
\label{sec:nested-observations}

A spatial transcriptomic tissue section contains $N$ measured positions indexed by $i\in\{1,\ldots,N\}$. Each position $i$ is associated with a position-level molecular profile $x_i\in\mathbb{R}^{G}$, containing expression measurements over $G$ genes, and a spatial coordinate $p_i\in\mathbb{R}^{2}$. Depending on the spatial transcriptomics platform, a measured position may correspond to a cell, spot, or spatial bin.

For each focal position $i$, we construct a nested neighborhood $S_{i,r}$ containing $i$ and its $r-1$ nearest spatial neighbors, where neighborhood size $r$ defines the spatial granularity of the tissue context, with larger $r$ corresponding to broader spatial scales. We use a discrete set of granularities $r\in\mathcal{R}=\{8,32,128\}$, yielding nested tissue contexts centered at the same focal position. The corresponding granularity-level molecular profile is obtained by averaging the preprocessed expression profiles within each neighborhood, $m_{i,r}=\frac{1}{r}\sum_{j\in S_{i,r}}\bar{x}_j$.
Both position-level and granularity-level molecular profiles are subsequently tokenized into fixed-length sequences of 512 gene tokens, with each token encoding a gene identity and its discretized expression rank, before being passed to the ZoomST encoders.

\subsection{Molecular--Spatial Dual-View Representation Learning}
\label{sec:within-granularity}

At each spatial granularity $r$, ZoomST learns complementary spatial and molecular representations of the same tissue context. For the spatial view, a position-level molecular Transformer first encodes each molecular profile $x_j$, $j\in S_{i,r}$, into a latent state $h_j=E_{\mathrm{pos}}(\mathrm{Tokenize}(x_j))$.
A shared spatial Transformer then contextualizes these states within $S_{i,r}$ using their relative spatial coordinates and a granularity identifier. The contextualized states are subsequently aggregated by a shared DeepSets encoder~\citep{zaheer2017deepsets} to obtain the spatial representation $g_{i,r}\in\mathbb{R}^{d}$.

For the molecular view, the granularity-level molecular profile $m_{i,r}$ is directly encoded by a separate molecular Transformer, $e_{i,r}=E_{\mathrm{gran}}(\mathrm{Tokenize}(m_{i,r}))\in\mathbb{R}^{d}$.
Both representations use $d=256$. The position-level and granularity-level molecular Transformers have separate parameters while sharing gene and expression-rank embeddings.

The dual-view representations are jointly pretrained through masked expression reconstruction and within-granularity VICReg alignment~\citep{bardes2022vicreg}.
