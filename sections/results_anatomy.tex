\paragraph{Corresponding descriptions retain different anatomical uses.}
\label{sec:anatomical-scale-preference}
To examine why the granularities remain separately useful, we compare $g_8$ and $g_{128}$ with matched readouts on the same six-region visceral domain of E14.5 E1S1. Broader context recovers the Lung more completely, increasing IoU from 0.894 to 0.955. Smooth muscle shows the opposite preference: fine context better separates its narrow bands and rings, whereas coarse context includes more surrounding tissue, lowering IoU from 0.779 to 0.580 (Figure~\ref{fig:results1-mosta}; protocol in Appendix~\ref{app:scale-cases}).

The native-state analysis shows improved cross-granularity correspondence, while this anatomical case shows that the retained granularities are not interchangeable. An analysis can use broader context for regional identity or finer context for narrow compartments without retraining the encoder.
