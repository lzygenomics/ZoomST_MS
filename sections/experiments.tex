\section{Evaluation design}
\label{sec:experiments}

\paragraph{Data and evaluation protocol.}
We pretrain separate models on 45 MOSTA mouse embryo sections~\citep{chen2022mosta} and 68 HESTA human embryo sections~\citep{pan2026hesta}, reserving eight sections spanning E9.5--E16.5 and seven spanning CS12--13 to CS23, respectively.
Supervision uses only granularities $r \in \{8,32,128\}$.
Held-out evaluations freeze the encoders and transformation; input preprocessing and target-fitted readouts follow Appendices~\ref{app:preprocessing} and~\ref{app:scale-cases}.

\paragraph{Representation evaluation.}
We compare anatomical clustering and physical 32-neighbor retention against frozen pretrained and target-fitted methods, sharing evaluation positions, annotation-defined cluster counts, and clustering readouts within each benchmark.
At each granularity, Joint concatenates $e_r$ and $g_r$; combined-granularity Joint concatenates these features across all three trained granularities.
Brain developmental information is assessed through diffusion-pseudotime--stage correlations and leave-one-section-out linear stage prediction.
Ablations match total training updates while omitting cross-granularity supervision (\emph{No Cross}) or, on MOSTA, JointMMD alone (\emph{CS}).

\paragraph{Transformation and response evaluation.}
On held-out MOSTA sections, we test trained granularity relations and unseen targets $r \in \{12,16,24,48,64,96\}$ against directly encoded neighborhood states.
Unseen-target predictions start from $e_8$ without access to target encodings.
Shuffled-pair controls assess source--target associations, while comparisons of directly encoded changes between low- and high-predicted-response locations assess relative context sensitivity.
Regional IoU and anatomical cases relate granularity preferences and responses to tissue organization; cases drawn from training sections are identified separately.
Table~\ref{tab:evaluation_protocol} summarizes data use, and Appendices~\ref{app:scale-cases}--\ref{app:objective-ablation} provide the evaluation protocols.
